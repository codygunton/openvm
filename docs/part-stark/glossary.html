Defined Terms#

Verifying Key (VK)

Contains per-AIR parameters: preprocessed commitment, constraint DAG, interaction definitions, quotient degree. The VK’s pre_hash is the first thing absorbed into the transcript (MultiStarkVerifyingKey).

MMCS (Mixed-Matrix Commitment Scheme)

A Merkle tree that commits multiple matrices of different heights in a single tree. Shorter matrices are injected at higher levels (_build_mmcs_tree).

Air ID

Integer identifier for each AIR in a multi-AIR proof. Must be unique and sorted in the proof.

Exposed Values

The cumulative sums from the LogUp after-challenge computation, revealed to the verifier. Their sum across all AIRs must equal zero.
